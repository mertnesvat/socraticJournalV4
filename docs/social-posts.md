# Rumi Breathing — Short Video Content Playbook

*TikTok · Instagram Reels · YouTube Shorts*

---

## The Core Content Strategy

Every short video for Rumi Breathing should follow this formula:

```
Hook (0–3s)     → Stop the scroll with a surprising claim or challenge
Payoff (3–45s)  → Deliver the science or demonstration — earn the trust
CTA (last 3s)   → Soft sell or curiosity loop back to the app
```

The brand voice is **curious and authoritative, never salesy**. You are the person who read the book so others don't have to — then made the tool to actually do it.

## BOLT SCORE — The Highest-Converting Angle

The BOLT score is the single best piece of short-form content in the app. It has:
- An interactive challenge element (viewers can try it right now)
- A counter-intuitive result (most people score badly and are shocked)
- A scientific explanation that feels like a secret
- A direct link to anxiety, sleep, and performance — topics the algorithm loves

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Visual identity for video:**
- Warm cream background (`#FAF7F2`) when showing app — matches app aesthetic, cohesive brand
- Teal text overlays (`#2D5F5D`) for key numbers and science terms
- No busy stock footage — talking head + screen recording is more credible than produced B-roll
- Show the BOLT timer counting up in real time — one of the most compelling visuals in the content

**Captions:**
- Always on — most mobile viewing is silent
- Key numbers in large text overlays: "12 seconds → 31 seconds", "5.5 BPM", "4,820%"
- Science terms subtitled as they're spoken

**Algorithm notes:**
- BOLT challenge videos that ask for scores in comments will generate comment velocity — the algorithm loves it
- "Drop your number below" posts consistently outperform pure education posts
- Controversy posts (mouth breathing, "doctors don't know this") have high share rates — people send them to partners and family members
